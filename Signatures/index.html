  1. 1 Click 🖥️ Copy for Outlook Web / Desktop on any template card above.
  2. 2 Open Outlook Desktop (Windows or Mac).
  3. 3 Go to File → Options → Mail → Signatures…
  4. 4 Click New, enter a name (e.g. "SaniXperts Meridian"), click OK.
  5. 5 Click inside the large signature editor area at the bottom.
  6. 6 Press Ctrl + V to paste the copied signature.
  7. 7 Click OK to save. Assign it to accounts as needed.
Tip: On Mac, use ⌘V to paste. The signature will render correctly with all formatting.
  1. 1 Click 📱 Copy for Outlook Mobile on any card (all cards copy the same plain-text version).
  2. 2 Open the Outlook mobile app on your iPhone or Android.
  3. 3 Tap your profile picture / initials in the top-left corner.
  4. 4 Tap the Settings gear ⚙ at the bottom-left.
  5. 5 Tap your email account under the Mail section.
  6. 6 Tap Signature.
  7. 7 Clear the existing text, then long-press and Paste.
  8. 8 Tap the checkmark ✓ or Save to confirm.
Note: Outlook Mobile only supports plain text signatures — rich HTML formatting is not available on mobile.
