# SaniXpert Digital Guide - Give & Go Facility

Welcome to the SaniXpert Digital Checklist. This system ensures compliance and safety for every deep-cleaning cycle.

## Step 1: Login & Identity (Face ID)
- Open the launcher and select your area (MACY Cupcake).
- Align your face in the camera prompt.
- First time users will be prompted to register name and role.

## Step 2: Pre-Cleaning (Bag Count)
- Complete the pre-clean checklist.
- Enter the exact number of covering bags used.
- Each checklist response requires a live camera photo.
- Once submitted, the Pre-Cleaning button vanishes to lock the stage.

## Step 3: Damage Reporting (Anytime)
- Use the red Damage Report gate at any time.
- Severity:
  - Low/Medium: dashboard notification only.
  - High: triggers a red alert and email.
- Each report requires a timestamped photo.

## Step 4: Post-Cleaning (Verification)
- Enter the number of bags retrieved.
- The count must match the Pre-Cleaning bag count.
- Mismatches are blocked until corrected.

## Step 5: Handover & Lead Sign-Off
- Complete each handover task with a camera photo.
- Area Lead must verify via Face ID.
- Lead signature is required to release the line.

## Emergency / Admin Unlock (PIN 2451)
- If a stage button vanished by mistake, use Help / Unlock.
- Enter PIN 2451 to restore a hidden stage for corrections.
